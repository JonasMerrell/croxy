Engineering flags at 150 °C

  1. Rail vs plate expansion: resolved. The top plate, floor plate and beam backers are all steel, so they grow with the rails. Z rails on 3030 aluminium posts still mismatch over 340 mm, so those need steel strips under the rails or mounts fixed at one end.
  2. Steel plate stability. Hot-rolled plate holds rolling stress and moves when you machine it. Rough both faces, stress-relieve (or heat-cycle to about 200 °C a few times), then finish-machine. Blacken or paint everything except the mating faces.
  3. Motors on a hot plate. The NEMA23 faces would conduct heat straight from a 150 °C plate. The sketch puts them on G10 or PEEK thermal-break standoffs with a ventilated top bay. The double-shear lower bearing sits under the plate.
  4. Fans in the chamber. Normal server fans and PTC heater fans won't survive 150 °C. The sketch keeps both fan motors outside: part-cooling air is ducted in from the top bay, and the heater uses a shaft-through convection impeller.
  5. Belts at the hottest spot. The XY belts run just under the plate, where the air is hottest. Check the Gates EPDM rated maximum temperature.
  6. PIR foam limit. Many PIR boards are rated near or below 150 °C continuous. Check your board's datasheet. A thin high-temp hot-face layer such as ceramic fiber or aerogel may be needed.
  7. Hot carbon gives fumes back. Activated carbon releases what it adsorbed when it's hot, so the filter loop cools the air before it reaches the filter.
  8. Beacon in a hot chamber. Confirm Beacon's rated operating temperature for a 150 °C chamber.
  9. Driver count. The Kraken's 8 drivers fit 4 XY + 3 Z + 1 E exactly, with none spare.

Decisions before modeling

A. XY motors.Decided: four NEMA23s, one per belt, pinwheeled around the corners.
B. Frame inside the insulation?The sketch puts the posts and plates inside the insulation so they heat evenly, with insulation and an outer skin wrapped around them.
C. Top plate thickness.Decided: mild steel. Proposed: 12 mm (about 11 kg). 10 mm would save weight if the sim allows it.
D. Small items.Decided: NEMA17 for Z, and an insulated front door with a double-glazed window. Proposed: a 200 × 200 bed.
